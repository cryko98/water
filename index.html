03 How it works

A coin with plumbing.

A memecoin can't dig a well by itself. But it can make sure the money it generates flows somewhere that does. No middleman, no "we'll donate later".

01

Trade

Every buy and sell of $WATER on Solana generates creator rewards. Volume is the rainfall.

02

Flow

Creator rewards are routed on-chain through donate.gg and converted to USDC. Locked in, automatic.

03

Pour

The USDC settles to WaterAid America, which works with local partners on clean water, toilets and hygiene.

04

Prove

Every settled receipt is public. The Reservoir reads them live and shows the full trail, nothing hidden.
